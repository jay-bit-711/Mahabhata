Anukramanika — The Sauti arrives
[Image · Sauti approaching the sages]

SANSKRIT

नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम्।
देवीं सरस्वतीं चैव ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ॥

[GIF · Bowing to Narayana]

ENGLISH

Having bowed to Narayana and Nara the foremost of men, and to Sarasvati, then let Jaya be uttered.

Ugrasrava Sauti arrives at the Naimisha satra of Shaunaka and is asked to tell the great Bharata.
