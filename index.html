Adi Parva · Adhyaya 1

Anukramanika Parva — The Arrival at Naimisha

Sanskrit

नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम्।
देवीं सरस्वतीं चैव ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ॥

English

Having bowed to Narayana, and to Nara the supreme among men, and to the goddess Sarasvati, then let Jaya be uttered.

Ugrasrava, son of Lomaharshana, a Sauti skilled in the Puranas, approached with humility the great sages of firm vows seated at ease in the Naimisha forest at the twelve-year satra of Kulapati Shaunaka.

The sages welcomed him and requested the narration of the great Bharata.
