Adi Parva · Adhyaya 1

Anukramanika Parva — Arrival at Naimisha

SANSKRIT

नारायणं नमस्कृत्य नरं चैव नरोत्तमम्।
देवीं सरस्वतीं चैव ततो जयमुदीरयेत् ॥

ENGLISH

Having bowed to Narayana and to Nara the foremost of men, and to the goddess Sarasvati, then let the word of victory be spoken.

In the Naimisha forest, at the twelve-year sacrifice of Kulapati Shaunaka, the sages sat in ease. Ugrasrava, son of Lomaharshana, a Sauti learned in the Puranas, approached them with humility.

They welcomed him and asked him to narrate the great history of the Bharatas.
